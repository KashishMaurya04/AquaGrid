Network-Aware Water Distribution Optimization Notice

This module evaluates physical delivery feasibility across pipe conduits under maximum capacity constraints using graph algorithms (Dijkstra Shortest-Path & Priority-Queue Widest-Path). It simulates steady-state conduit flow and does not perform dynamic multi-commodity pressure or transient wave hydraulic modeling. Historical assessment records preserve immutable snapshots captured at assessment time.
