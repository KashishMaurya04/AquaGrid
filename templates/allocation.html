Fairness Doctrine & Allocation Mechanics

Transparent, explainable resource rationing under the three operational supply regimes.

DSA Max-Min Fairness

● Scenario 1: Abundant Supply

When aggregate municipal supply meets or exceeds total requested demand across all zones, every zone receives 100% of its requested volume.

Supply S ≥ Σ d_i ⟹ a_i = d_i (100%)

● Scenario 2: Constrained Supply

When supply covers critical baselines but cannot fulfill all demand, essential minimums are guaranteed first. Remaining surplus is leveled via progressive water-filling.

Step 1: a_i = e_i
Step 2: Level (x_i / w_i) on (d_i - e_i)

● Scenario 3: Severe Emergency Deficit

When available supply falls below aggregate survival needs, water is rationed strictly proportionally to essential requirements to sustain human life.

Supply S < Σ e_i ⟹ a_i = S · (e_i / Σ e_j)
Fairness Weights vs. Priority Categories: Fairness weights ($w_i > 0$) determine the proportional leveling slope when allocating residual water during constrained supply (Scenario 2). A zone with weight $2.0\times$ receives twice the residual water per leveling unit compared to a zone with weight $1.0\times$, until its requested demand ceiling is reached. Priority categories (Critical, Standard, Commercial, Agricultural) serve as administrative and operational metadata tags; they do not automatically override numerical fairness weights unless configured.
